# Decisions and next proofs

## Selected direction

- Persistent open-world mining ARPG with crafting and equipment management in the world.
- Fine-scale material terrain and organic, hierarchical whole-world generation.
- WFC for useful feature constraints within the hierarchy; continuous geography across logical regions.
- Direct pixel art with composable equipment, animation layers, effects, and material variants.
- Many visually distinct biomes within a coherent art style.
- Project-owned builds; Userland deployment with content-addressed artifact reuse.

## Next proofs

- **Rendering:** synchronized equipment swaps, per-frame occlusion, effects, and lighting in-engine.
- **World:** character traversal, chunk seams, persistent digs, and selected material behaviors.
- **Mobile:** touch feel, readability, frame time, memory, and lifecycle recovery on real devices.

## Open choices

Engine/version and browser support floor; final lighting and authoring tools; detailed simulation rules; death/recovery and overlay pause behavior; online progression authority; code and asset licenses.

Use short numbered decision records when a consequential technical choice is settled: decision, rationale, evidence, and consequences.
